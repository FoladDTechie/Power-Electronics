
## Main current path
- XT60: 30 A continuous, 60 A burst, contact 0.55 mOhm -> design target 30 A / 60 A
- 2 x IPT012N08N5 at 30 A (continuous): 0.54 W at RDS(on) max 25 C, 0.92 W with x1.7 hot [ASSUMPTION]; per FET 0.46 W -> +18 K at RthJA 40 K/W (6 cm2 copper)
- 2 x IPT012N08N5 at 60 A (burst): 2.16 W at RDS(on) max 25 C, 3.67 W with x1.7 hot [ASSUMPTION]; per FET 1.84 W -> +73 K at RthJA 40 K/W (6 cm2 copper)
- Shunt 0.5 mOhm at 30 A: 0.45 W vs 1.0 W rating (continuous limit from P: 44.7 A)
- Shunt 0.5 mOhm at 60 A: 1.80 W vs 1.0 W rating (continuous limit from P: 44.7 A)
- Board path without copper: 2.20 mOhm -> 66 mV drop at 30 A (PCB copper not included)

## INA226 scaling
- Shunt full scale 81.92 mV / 0.5 mOhm = 163.8 A; shunt LSB 5 mA
- Current_LSB = 2 mA -> CAL = 0.00512 / (2 mA x 0.5 mOhm) = 5120 (0x1400); register limit 65.5 A; power LSB 50 mW
- Offset 10 uV max = 20 mA; gain 0.1 % + shunt 1 % + TCR 350 ppm/K (x 50 K self-heating = 1.75 %) [ASSUMPTION 50 K]
- At 30 A: worst case about +-0.88 A
- INA226 is powered from the flight controller's 3.3 V: SDA/SCL VIH = 0.7 x VS, so a 5 V supply would not accept 3.3 V logic

## Anti-spark: gate supply
- VBAT 12.0 V: zener current up to 0.03 mA; DISARM short draws 0.55 mA, 7 mW in R_SUP
- VBAT 16.8 V: zener current up to 0.25 mA; DISARM short draws 0.76 mA, 13 mW in R_SUP
- VBAT 25.2 V: zener current up to 0.63 mA; DISARM short draws 1.15 mA, 29 mW in R_SUP
- Gate node <= 12.7 V (BZX84-C12 max at 5 mA) vs VGS max 20 V. [ASSUMPTION] at 0.1-0.6 mA the zener sits a little below its 5 mA value
- Disarmed but plugged in: 1.15 mA at 25.2 V = 27 mAh/day

## Anti-spark: precharge
- R_PRE = 2S2P of 10 Ohm CRGP2512 = 10 Ohm; C_LOAD 2200 uF [ASSUMPTION] -> tau 22 ms, 99 % at 101 ms
- At 25.2 V: peak 2.52 A, peak power 63.5 W (15.9 W per resistor), energy 0.70 J (0.175 J per resistor)
- CRGP2512 has a pulse graph only (2 W, 500 V); 0.18 J in ~22 ms is NOT checked against it
- ESC idle 0.2 A [ASSUMPTION] leaves 2.0 V across R_PRE when the main FETs close
- Without precharge the plug-in current is limited only by lead and capacitor impedance (see sim/results.txt)

## Anti-spark: main FET delay
- nominal : tau 1.04 s, VGS reaches VTH 3.0 V at 298 ms, 10 V at 1.86 s
- earliest: tau 0.92 s, VGS reaches VTH 2.2 V at 174 ms, 10 V at 1.42 s
- latest  : tau 1.15 s, VGS reaches VTH 3.8 V at 465 ms, 10 V at 2.41 s
- Precharge 99 % (101 ms) comes before the earliest VTH crossing
- The BECs must not start before the latest VTH crossing, or their load lands on R_PRE: see the EN delay below (C_EN -10 % included there)
- Turn-off on DISARM: 1 uF through 100 Ohm + 1N4148W -> tau 0.1 ms; peak 127 mA (1N4148W IFSM 500 mA)
- Disarming under load would run the FETs through their linear region for ~0.1 ms: only disarm with motors stopped

## Low-voltage alarm (MCP65R41, VREF 1.21 V)
- 4S (JP1 open): alarm ON below 14.01 V (3.50 V/cell, worst case 13.36-14.67 V), OFF above 14.59 V (3.65 V/cell)
    filter: 1 uF x 171k = 171 ms
- 6S (JP1 closed): alarm ON below 21.06 V (3.51 V/cell, worst case 20.07-22.06 V), OFF above 21.93 V (3.65 V/cell)
    filter: 1 uF x 114k = 114 ms
- With the 5 V rail off and 25.2 V on the bus, VIN- input current = 12.4 uA vs 2 mA abs max input current
- VREF load 1.2 uA vs +-500 uA; no capacitor on VREF (datasheet: > 2000 pF may oscillate)
- Buzzer: 30 mA through 2N7002 (5.3 Ohm max at 4.5 V) -> 4.84 V across it (operating 4.0-7.0 V)

## Quiescent drain
- Armed, before BEC load, at 25.2 V: about 0.79 mA from dividers and gate supply, plus BEC no-load current (not computed)
